      How to play

      • Expand: click any land you don't own to send troops at it. Grey land is cheap. Defended land is not.
      • Grow: land raises your troop cap, with diminishing returns the wider you sprawl. A huge nation still needs cities to keep growing.
      • Earn: gold trickles in on its own and stacks with ports and trade routes. Land itself pays no gold.
      • Surround: anything you completely box in falls to you for free. Annex a nation this way and you take its whole treasury.
      • Build: cities raise your troop cap, ports earn trade gold, defense posts make your land expensive to take.
      • Escalate: silos launch nukes, SAMs shoot them down, and boats carry invasions across the sea.
      • Scheme: ally with rivals for protection and richer trade. Then betray them, if you can afford the reputation.
      • Win: hold 60% of the world's land.
